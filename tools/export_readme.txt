HUNDRED GATES - early test build (Windows)

HOW TO START
  1. Extract the whole zip to a folder first. Do not run it from inside the
     zip.
  2. Run HundredGates.exe. Nothing else needs to be installed.
  Windows may say it protected your PC, because the file is not signed.
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway". On a PC managed by a company or school
  that choice may be missing; use another PC.

HOW TO PLAY
  You start in the town. Walk up into the green gate at the top to enter
  the tower. Clear every room of a floor, then beat its boss.
  To stop: pause, choose "Quit to title" and confirm, then "Quit". Or close
  the window.

CONTROLS (keyboard and mouse)
  Move ........ W A S D or the arrow keys
  Aim ......... the mouse: you aim at the cursor
  Attack ...... left mouse button or J
  Dodge ....... Space
  Pause ....... Escape
  In the menus: W S or Up Down move; Enter, Space or E choose; Escape goes
  back; the mouse can click.

CONTROLS (gamepad)
  Move ........ left stick or D-pad
  Aim ......... right stick
  Attack ...... X button (the left one of the four)
  Dodge ....... A button (the bottom one)
  Pause ....... Start
  In the menus: stick or D-pad move; A or Y choose; B goes back.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Progress is not saved yet. Quitting to the title or closing the game
    starts over.
  - Everything on screen is placeholder art. The look is not final.
  - Only two settings are kept: screen shake and fullscreen. Their file is
    %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Hundred Gates\settings.cfg
    Delete it to go back to the defaults.
